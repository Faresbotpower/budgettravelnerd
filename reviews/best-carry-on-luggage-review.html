Buying Guide: How to Choose the Best Carry-On Luggage

Size and Airline Compliance

Before anything else, make sure your carry-on meets airline size requirements. Most domestic airlines allow bags up to 22" x 14" x 9", but international carriers may have stricter limits. Always check with your specific airline, especially for budget carriers that often have smaller restrictions.

Hard Shell vs. Soft Shell

Hardshell luggage offers better protection for fragile items and is generally more water-resistant. However, soft shell bags are often lighter, more flexible for slight overpacking, and can be more affordable. Consider your packing style and what you typically travel with when making this decision.

Wheels: Two vs. Four

Four-wheel spinner bags can roll upright beside you and are easier to maneuver in tight spaces. Two-wheel bags are typically more stable when stopped and may have larger wheel wells, but they must be tilted when rolling. For most travelers, four wheels are the way to go.

Weight Considerations

Every pound matters when you're traveling, especially if you're close to airline weight limits. Look for bags under 8 pounds if possible, but don't sacrifice too much durability for weight savings. The lightest bags in our test were the Travelpro Maxlite 5 at just 5.1 pounds.

Interior Organization

Consider how you pack and what features will help you stay organized. Some travelers prefer simple, open compartments, while others want multiple pockets, compression zippers, and dividers. Think about whether you need dedicated laptop compartments or external pockets for quick access items.

Handle Quality

You'll be grabbing, pulling, and adjusting your carry-on handle countless times. Look for sturdy construction with multiple height positions. Telescoping handles should extend and retract smoothly without wobbling when extended.

Wheel Quality

Good wheels can make the difference between effortless gliding and constant fighting with your bag. Look for larger wheels that can handle various surfaces, and ensure they're securely attached. In our testing, the Away and Samsonite options had the smoothest-rolling wheels.

Warranty and Brand Support

Luggage takes a beating during travel, so warranty coverage is important. Some brands like Away and Briggs & Riley offer lifetime warranties on certain components. Consider how the brand handles customer service and repair claims when making your decision.

Price vs. Value

While it's tempting to go with the cheapest option, remember that quality luggage is an investment. A well-made bag that lasts for years is often more economical than replacing cheap luggage repeatedly. That said, you don't need to spend $400+ unless you're a very frequent traveler who will benefit from premium features.

Special Features

Modern carry-ons often include features like built-in USB ports, compression systems, removable batteries, or specialized compartments. While these can be convenient, prioritize basic quality and functionality over gadgets that may break or become obsolete.

Ultimately, the best carry-on luggage is the one that fits your specific travel style, budget, and needs.
